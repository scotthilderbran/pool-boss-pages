Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 (draft, before release)

This policy explains what the Pool Boss: Cleaning Empire game (“the game”) does with information. The game is published by Gregory Hilderbran Jr (“we”, “us”).

The short version: there are no accounts and we never ask for your name or email. Your progress is saved on your device. The game shows optional ads, and the ad partners may use your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

Information we collect

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details, and it has no accounts. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Information that stays on your device

The game keeps the following on your device only. We never receive it.

  • Your game progress: coins, gems, pools cleaned, upgrades and your business.
  • Your settings, such as sound and haptics.

Your device backups, which Apple manages, may include this data. You can remove it by deleting the game.

Information handled by others

Advertising. The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which works with other advertising networks. To show and measure ads, these companies may receive technical information such as your device model, operating system, IP address and how you interact with ads. They receive your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks; you can change this any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK), the game asks for your consent before ads that use your data, and you can change your choice later in the game’s Settings. Unity’s privacy policy and those of its partner networks govern that information.

Game analytics. We may use an analytics service to see anonymous gameplay events, such as which pool you reached and when a session started, so we can balance and improve the game. These events are not linked to your name or contact details. [Analytics provider to be named before release.]

Purchases. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing details. Apple’s privacy policy governs that information.

We do not sell your information, and we share it only with the partners described above for the purposes described above.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep no personal information on our own servers. Information on your device is removed by deleting the game. To ask that advertising or analytics data linked to your device be deleted, contact us and we will pass the request on to the partners involved.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

If you live somewhere with data-protection rights (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, or to object to its use for advertising. Contact us to make a request and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the current version is always on this page.
